Mobile-first

74% of live-tennis.eu's traffic is mobile. Every component collapses gracefully: rankings table drops the "Played" column < 768px, search becomes a full-screen sheet, the sticky header keeps the brand and the search trigger only.

  • Touch-friendly 44px tap targets
  • prefers-reduced-motion respected globally
  • Skeletons not spinners on async sections
  • Font-display: swap (no FOIT)
  • OG cards generated at the Edge for share previews
