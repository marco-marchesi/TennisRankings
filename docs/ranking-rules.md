# ATP & WTA Ranking Rules (2026)

> Sources: ATP 2026 Official Rulebook Chapter 9 (PDF), WTA 2026 Rulebook (PDF), ITF 2026 Points Table, ATP Rankings FAQ, WTA Rankings Explained, Wikipedia cross-checks.

---

## ATP Rankings

### Core mechanic

Rolling **52-week cumulative** system. Points drop exactly 52 weeks after being earned. Exception: ATP Finals points expire on the Monday after the last regular ATP event of the *following* calendar year.

Used for tournament entry and seeding. The separate **Race to Turin** (calendar-year reset) is used only for ATP Finals qualification.

---

### How many results count (2026 — changed from 19 to 18)

| Slot | Events |
|------|--------|
| 4 | All 4 Grand Slams (mandatory if accepted) |
| 8 | 8 mandatory ATP Masters 1000 events |
| 1 | Nitto ATP Finals (bonus 19th slot if qualified) |
| **6** | **Best 6 remaining** (Monte Carlo, ATP 500s, ATP 250s, Challenger, ITF, United Cup) |
| **Total** | **18 (+1 Finals bonus)** |

For 2025 and prior the total was 19 (7 "best others"), now 18 (6 "best others"). A player who qualifies for Turin fills 4 + 8 + 5 best others + Finals = 18.

### Mandatory tournaments

#### ATP Masters 1000 — 8 of 9 mandatory

| Tournament | Surface |
|------------|---------|
| Indian Wells Open | Hard |
| Miami Open | Hard |
| Madrid Open | Clay |
| Italian Open (Rome) | Clay |
| Canadian Open (Montreal/Toronto alternating) | Hard |
| Cincinnati Open | Hard |
| Shanghai Masters | Hard |
| Paris Masters | Hard (indoor) |

**Monte-Carlo Masters is NOT mandatory** (optional since 2009). If played, it counts toward the 6 "best others".

#### ATP 500 — 4 mandatory per year (Top 30 players)

Top 30 players must play **4 ATP 500 events** per year, distributed across windows:
- At least 1 in the February–April window
- At least 1 in the May–August window
- At least 1 after the US Open (fall swing)

There are 16 ATP 500 events per year. Top 30 players receive guaranteed main draw entry.

---

### Penalties for missing mandatory tournaments

#### Missing a Grand Slam or mandatory Masters 1000 (Top 30)

If a Top 30 player is accepted into a mandatory event but does not play (without a valid medical excuse), the **zero-point result occupies one of the 18 counting slots**, displacing a positive result from elsewhere. There is no separate "extra zero forced in" rule — the unplayed event simply counts as 0 where a real result might have been.

Financial fine: up to **$60,000** per mandatory Masters 1000 violation. Fine doubles for consecutive violations within the same calendar year.

#### Missing a mandatory ATP 500

A Top 30 player who withdraws from a required ATP 500 event receives a **zero in that 500 slot**. One "repair play" is allowed: the player may substitute one additional ATP 500 played later in the calendar year to replace that zero. This repair option is available once per year.

Financial fine: up to **$40,000** per ATP 500 obligation violation.

#### Medical/injury exemption

Players absent **30+ consecutive days** due to documented injury or illness do not receive ranking penalties for mandatory events missed during that period.

#### Parental exemption (2026)

Players may withdraw within **14 days before or after** a child's birth or adoption without penalty.

---

### Protected ranking

A player injured/ill for **6+ months** may petition for a protected ranking within 6 months of their last tournament. Its value = average ranking during the first 3 months of absence.

| Absence duration | Events covered | Maximum time cap |
|------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| 6–12 months | First 9 singles + 9 doubles events | 9 months |
| 12+ months | First 12 singles + 12 doubles events | 12 months |

---

### Race to Turin (Nitto ATP Finals qualification)

Calendar-year standings (resets January 1). Used solely for Finals qualification.

#### Singles qualification (8 spots)

- Top **7** in the Race after the Paris Masters qualify automatically.
- **8th spot**: If one 2026 Grand Slam champion ranks 8th–20th in the Race → they qualify 8th. If two Grand Slam champions are in positions 8–20 → the higher-ranked of the two qualifies; the other is first alternate. If no 2026 Grand Slam champion is 8th–20th → the player ranked 8th qualifies.

#### Doubles qualification (8 teams)

Top 8 teams in the Race; same 8th-spot Slam-champion rule applies.

---

### Tiebreak rules (same points total)

1. Most combined points from Grand Slams + mandatory Masters 1000 + ATP Finals.
2. Fewest tournaments played in the 52-week window (missed mandatories count as played).
3. Highest single-result points, then second-highest, etc.

---

### Age/competition restrictions (juniors turning pro)

| Age | Maximum ATP/Challenger events per year |
|-----|----------------------------------------|
| 14 | 8 |
| 15 | 12 |
| 16+ | No restriction |

## WTA Rankings

### Core mechanic

Rolling **52-week cumulative** system. Points drop 52 weeks after being earned. Used for draw entry and seeding. The separate **Race to the WTA Finals** (calendar-year reset) is used only for Finals qualification.

To appear on the published rankings a player must have earned points at **at least 3 tournaments** OR earned a minimum of **10 singles ranking points** in total.

---

### How many results count

| Slot | Events |
|------|--------|
| 4 | All 4 Grand Slams |
| 6 | Best 6 of 7 **combined WTA 1000s** (Indian Wells, Miami, Madrid, Rome, Toronto/Montreal, Cincinnati, Beijing) |
| 1 | Best 1 of 3 **non-combined WTA 1000s** (Doha, Dubai, Wuhan) |
| 7 | Best 7 from remaining: WTA 1000, WTA 500, WTA 250, WTA 125, ITF |
| +1 | WTA Finals (**bonus 19th slot** if attended) |
| **Total** | **18 (+1 Finals bonus)** |

**Doubles:** Best 12 results. WTA Finals counts as one of the 12 (not a bonus).

### Mandatory tournaments

Since 2024, **all 10 WTA 1000 events are mandatory** for Top 10 players aged 18+.

**Combined WTA 1000s (7 — best 6 count):**
1. Indian Wells (BNP Paribas Open)
2. Miami Open
3. Madrid Open
4. Italian Open (Rome)
5. Canadian Open (Toronto/Montreal alternating)
6. Cincinnati Open
7. China Open (Beijing)

**Non-Combined WTA 1000s (3 — best 1 counts):**
8. Qatar Open (Doha)
9. Dubai Duty Free Tennis Championships
10. Wuhan Open

The mandatory obligation applies to players **ranked inside the Top 10 at the event's entry deadline date**.

---

### Penalties for missing WTA 1000 mandatory tournaments

- Player fails to play a mandatory WTA 1000 (Top 10, age 18+) → **zero-point result** for that tournament slot.
- Player opts out **before the main draw entry deadline** → **no penalty** (zero is not applied).
- Player opts out **after the deadline but plays an exhibition or non-WTA event in the same week** → **zero-point result penalty** applies.
- Player withdraws without having opted out after the deadline → **zero-point result**.

No explicit fixed-dollar fines for WTA 1000 non-compliance are publicly documented at the same level of specificity as ATP fines.

---

### WTA 500 obligations

No specific minimum number of WTA 500 events is mandated for Top 10 players. WTA 500 results count in the "best 7 remaining" calculation pool.

---

### Race to WTA Finals (qualification)

Calendar-year standings (resets January 1). Used solely for Finals qualification.

#### Points counted in the Race

Same 18-tournament composition as the ranking:
- 4 Grand Slams
- Best 6 of 7 combined WTA 1000s
- Best 1 of 3 non-combined WTA 1000s
- Best 7 remaining

#### Minimum participation requirement

Players must compete in at least **8 WTA 1000 or WTA 500 tournaments** during the season to qualify for the WTA Finals.

#### Singles qualification (8 spots)

- Top **7** in the Race after the last qualifying event qualify automatically.
- **8th spot**: Any player ranked 8th–20th in the Race who won a Grand Slam in the current calendar year qualifies 8th.

#### Doubles qualification (8 teams)

- Teams must compete together in at least **2 events** to appear on the doubles Race leaderboard.
- Top 8 teams qualify.

---

### Tiebreak rules (same points total)

1. Most combined points from Grand Slams + WTA 1000 Mandatory + WTA Finals.
2. Most total points from all WTA tournaments + Grand Slams.
3. Fewest tournaments played in the 52-week window.
4. Highest single-result points, then second-highest, etc.

---

### Special rules

- **Bye rule:** A player who receives a bye and loses their first match still receives first-round points.
- **Doubles:** Best 12 results; WTA Finals counts as one of the 12 (not a bonus 13th).
- **Protected ranking:** Available after extended injury/illness absence; used for entry and seeding for a limited number of events.
- **Olympics (2024 Paris):** WTA awarded Olympic points on a temporary basis (Gold = 750, Silver = 450, Bronze = 375). Olympic points policies vary by season and are subject to WTA approval.

## Key differences: ATP vs. WTA

| Feature | ATP | WTA |
|---------|-----|-----|
| Total results counted | 18 (+1 Finals bonus) | 18 (+1 Finals bonus) |
| Grand Slam finalist | 1,300 | 1,300 |
| Grand Slam SF | 800 | 780 |
| Grand Slam QF | 400 | 430 |
| Grand Slam R16 | 200 | 240 |
| Grand Slam R32 | 100 | 130 |
| Grand Slam qualifying R1 loss | 0 | 2 |
| 1000 winner | 1,000 | 1,000 (combined); 900 (non-combined) |
| Mandatory 1000s | 8 of 9 (Monte Carlo optional) | All 10 |
| 500 mandatory count | 4 per year (Top 30) | No minimum |
| Non-mandatory 1000 | Monte Carlo (counted as "best other") | Doha/Dubai/Wuhan (best 1 of 3 separate slot) |
| Team competition points | United Cup (since 2024) | None currently |
| Davis Cup / Billie Jean King Cup | No points since 2016 | No points |
