# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email **security@tennisrankings.example** with the details. We aim to
acknowledge reports within 72 hours and to ship a fix within 14 days for
high-severity issues.

Please do not file public GitHub issues for security reports.

## Scope

In scope:

- `tennisrankings.example` web application and APIs
- The scraper worker (subdomain TBD)

Out of scope:

- Third-party services we integrate with (Vercel, Neon, Cloudflare, Resend,
  Sentry, Plausible) — please report to those vendors directly.
- Denial-of-service via volumetric traffic.
- Findings derived from social engineering of staff or contributors.

## Safe-harbour

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who:

- Make a good-faith effort to avoid privacy violations, destruction of data,
  and degradation of services.
- Only interact with their own accounts.
- Give us reasonable time to respond before disclosing.

Thank you for helping keep TennisRankings safe.
